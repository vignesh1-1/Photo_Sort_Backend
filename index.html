Methodology & System Pipeline

Automated photo quality scoring based on computer vision processing rules.

1. Sharpness / Focus (Laplacian Variance)

Convolves the image with a standard 3x3 Laplacian edge-detection kernel to compute pixel intensity variances. High variance designates sharp focus, while low variance indicates blur.

2. Luminance & Exposure Histogram

Computes brightness distribution to identify underexposed shadows (<45) or clipped highlights (>210).

3. Eye Blink Ratio & Duplicate Hashing

Identifies mid-blink closed eyes and applies perceptual hashing to detect duplicate burst sequences.
