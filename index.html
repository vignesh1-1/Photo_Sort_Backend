System Specifications & Engineering Pipeline

PhotoSort combines computer vision, perceptual hashing, and client-server synchronization to automate photography culling.

1. Laplacian Variance Blur Detection

Convolves the image with a 3x3 Laplacian edge-detection kernel to compute pixel intensity variances. Sharp focus produces high second-derivative edge variance (>120), whereas lens defocus or motion blur flattens high-frequency gradients (<60).

2. Luminance Histogram & Clipping Mask

Computes pixel brightness across the 8-bit luminance spectrum. Underexposed shadows are flagged at mean values <45, while clipped highlight overexposure is flagged at >215. The in-modal clipping mask marks pure highlights in red and pure shadows in blue.

3. Haar Cascade Facial Landmark & Blink Detection

Applies frontal face Haar cascade classifiers to isolate subject faces, followed by eye-region cascade searches. If a clear face lacks detectable open eyes, the photo is penalized 20 points and flagged as "Closed Eyes / Blink".

4. 64-bit dHash Perceptual Hashing

Generates 64-bit difference hashes by scaling down images to 9x8 gradients. Sequences with a Hamming Distance <= 8 are classified as burst duplicates, automatically grouping burst shots and isolating the single sharpest hero frame.

5. Dual-Tier WebP Compressive Storage

To fit within MongoDB Atlas's 512 MB free tier quota, images are encoded into 1600px display WebP (82% quality) and 400px thumbnail WebP (70% quality), slashing typical 8 MB image footprints by 95% down to ~150 KB.

6. Lightroom-Style Keyboard Shortcuts

Accelerate manual review without leaving the keyboard:
• 1 or S: Mark Select
• 2 or R: Mark Review
• 3 or X: Mark Poor
• Arrow Left / Right: Next / Prev Image
• Spacebar: Toggle Inspection Modal
• Delete / Backspace: Trigger Custom Delete
